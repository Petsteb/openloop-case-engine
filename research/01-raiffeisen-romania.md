## 2. Customer complaints and operational failures

### 2.1 App and online banking outages
- **3 May 2023:** an outage lasting several hours hit Smart Mobile, Raiffeisen Online, SmartToken, Smart Business, the new Raiffeisen Online IMM, Open Banking (AISP/PISP/CISP), Google and Apple Pay enrolment, and the Smart Market enrolment flow. The maintenance window kept being extended. The bank did not explain the cause ([Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/economie/probleme-pentru-raiffeisen-bank-in-romania-nicio-2263639.html)).
- **29 Oct 2023:** a technical problem hit Smart Mobile, biometric online payments, MFM cash deposits and the website ([Economedia](https://economedia.ro/clientii-reclama-probleme-cu-serviciile-raiffeisen-bank-sambata-banca-echipa-tehnica-lucreaza-pentru-remedierea-situatiei.html)).
- **2024–2026:** I found **no dated outage in the news.** A StatusGator page has monitored Raiffeisen since Aug 2026 and shows an undated warning on Smart Mobile ([StatusGator](https://statusgator.com/services/raiffeisen-bank)). Treat outages as an older theme.

### 2.2 Account restrictions and KYC data refresh (the strongest recurring theme, but mostly anecdotal)
- **Generic, no bank named:** Capital.ro (20 May 2026) says banks restrict accounts after the KYC update deadline passes, and that "restricțiile sunt aplicate automat de sistemele informatice ale băncilor". The documents it lists are: ID, proof of address, employer, source of income and PEP declaration for individuals; for companies, a **recent ONRC certificate**, shareholder structure, administrator documents and activity information ([Capital.ro](https://www.capital.ro/multi-romani-risca-sa-ramana-fara-acces-la-banii-din-cont-banca-trimite-notificari-verificarile-pe-care-clientii-trebuie-sa-le-faca.html)).
- **Raiffeisen-specific but anecdotal:** Trustpilot rates the bank 1.4/5 on 95 self-selected reviews ([Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/raiffeisen.ro)):
  - 26 Oct 2026: "The app also didn't work today, so I couldn't update my details."
  - 18 Jun 2026: "The bank representative was not able to update my phone number for three weeks and I have my account locked."
  - 8 May 2025: "her accounts are blocked because she didn't update her personal details."
  - 7 Jul 2025: "decided to block my access to my own account. No warning. No reason."
- **2021:** "hundreds or even thousands" of accounts were closed without explanation. The ANPC Bucharest commissariat was reviewing the bank's General Business Conditions; I found no outcome ([Puterea.ro, 11 Feb 2021](https://www.puterea.ro/?p=34953)).
- Inference: the failures likely happen at the edges, such as phone or email changes that need a branch visit, documents that don't match, and company files. A search snippet suggested Smart Mobile already supports the periodic ID-data update, but I could not tie it to a citable page, so treat that as unverified.

### 2.3 Fraud and scams
- **Jan 2025:** Raiffeisen warned clients about spoofed calls from people posing as bank employees, and about transferring money to unknown recipients ([Capital.ro, 24 Jan 2025](https://www.capital.ro/avertisment-pentru-toti-clientii-raiffeisen-bank-se-goleste-contul-de-bani.html); [RomaniaTV](https://www.romaniatv.net/?p=8711014)) [2 src].
- **Insider fraud:** the ANSPDCP findings from 2022 and 2024 above are the strongest Raiffeisen-specific evidence. Staff opened products and changed client contact data without consent.
- **Anecdotal:** Trustpilot, 1 Jun 2026, on a fraud case: "10 days since that moment and no sign from their support."

### 2.4 Remediation and refunds handled manually
The 2021 ANPC case: the bank said the recalculation was "relativ complex" and processed "manual" (see §1). Refunds on the separate interest-clause case were still incomplete in 2024 (see §1).

### 2.5 Searched, nothing Raiffeisen-specific found
- Garnishment (poprire) handling errors. Only generic ANAF wrongful-garnishment stories exist, e.g. [Playtech 2024](https://playtech.ro/2024/romanul-cu-datorii-zero-care-s-a-trezit-cu-toate-conturile-blocate-poprirea-gresita-de-la-anaf-tradeaza-realitatea-din-romania/).
- Slow mortgage processing.
- Chargebacks and disputed card transactions.
- Branch queues.
- Per-bank CSALB statistics. System-wide, CSALB requests rose 40% in H1 2026 vs H1 2025 ([ZF](https://www.zf.ro/banci-si-asigurari/romanii-au-transmis-la-csalb-in-s1-2026-peste-2-000-de-cereri-de-23195619)) [1 src].

**Recurring themes:**
1. Problems updating client data and KYC that end in account locks.
2. Weak controls on staff changes to client data and products.
3. Data sent to the wrong person or the wrong file.
4. Manual, slow remediation work.
5. Slow support response.

## 5. Romanian rule-heavy, document-heavy processes that create bank workload

### 5.1 Popriri (garnishments): ANAF channel, with hard numbers
**ANAF H1 2025 activity report** [primary] ([ANAF PDF, 18 Aug 2025](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/Informatii_R/18.08.2025%20-%20Raport%20de%20activitateANAF_2025S1_V7.pdf)):
- Through **e-Popriri**, ANAF sent credit institutions **2,250,489 enforcement acts** in H1 2025. This is **system-wide, across all banks.** The breakdown:
  - **1,079,509** garnishment orders on bank balances
  - **1,125,352** lifting decisions (this count includes some acts communicated up to end-2024)
  - 26,631 suspension notices
  - 18,997 continuation notices
- **2.92bn lei** was collected from garnishments of bank accounts (+22.89% year on year).
- The separate figure of 581,804 garnishment addresses concerns *amounts owed by third parties*, not bank accounts.
- There is no breakdown by bank, so do not derive a Raiffeisen share.

**OPANAF 626/2023**, in force from 1 Oct 2023:
- Banks must report via e-Popriri how much can actually be paid from a debtor's accounts, both after a garnishment is set up and whenever money arrives. The goal is to stop over-collection when a debtor has accounts at several banks.
- Transfers made during a loaded suspension are not validated by the Treasury and are returned.
- Sources: [ANAF order PDF](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/OPANAF_626_2023.pdf); [Fiscalitatea](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/e-popriri-din-1-octombrie-2023-bancile-ar-putea-fi-obligate-sa-comunice-detalii-despre-sumele-care-pot-fi-platite-din-conturile-datornicilor-22634/); [Contabilul](https://contabilul.manager.ro/a/27647/bancile-vor-raporta-catre-anaf-informatii-despre-sumele-din-conturile-celor-popriti.html) [2 src].

**Raiffeisen link:** popriri processing sits in the Brașov operations centre (2013, see §3.2). I found **no Raiffeisen-specific garnishment error.**

**Bailiff (executor judecătoresc) popriri:** these fall under the Code of Civil Procedure, art. 781 and following. **I found nothing on the delivery channel or format.** Court practice gives conflicting deadlines for the garnishee bank, 15 days in one ruling and 5 days under art. 786 in another, so check the current text ([Legeaz art. 782](https://legeaz.net/noul-cod-de-procedura-civila/art-782); [Decizia 85/2013](https://legeaz.net/spete-comercial-litigii-profesionisti-cluj-2013/decizia-civila-85-2013-cluj-x4o); [Jurisprudence 515/2017](https://legeaz.net/spete-civil-2/validare-poprire-jurisprudenta-popriri-515-2017)).

**Unseizable amounts (sume insesizabile) thresholds:** no reliable source found. Do not hard-code values without checking the Code of Civil Procedure and the Fiscal Procedure Code.

### 5.2 ANAF account and FATCA/CRS reporting
- Since 2016 banks have reported **daily** to ANAF which accounts were opened and closed (OPANAF 3770/2015). ANAF can request turnover, balances and supporting documents on demand ([Alba24](https://alba24.ro/obligatii-pentru-banci-de-la-1-ianuarie-trebuie-sa-raporteze-zilnic-catre-anaf-situatia-conturilor-deschise-sau-inchise-de-clienti-461686.html)) [1 src].
- FATCA and CRS are reported on **F3000 forms** (DAC2/CRS and FATCA versions, amended by OPANAF 1711/2022). The 2025 deadline was 15 May. ANAF said it would control both timeliness and the **correctness and integrity** of F3000 data. Its DAC2/CRS/FATCA guide was updated on 29.12.2025. Client self-certification uses a "Fișă Client" for CRS and W-9 / W-8BEN forms for FATCA.
- Sources: [ANAF guide](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/AsistentaContribuabili_r/AEOI/Ghid%20DAC2%20CRS%20FATCAVARIANTA%2029.12.2025.pdf); [Fiscalitatea](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/formularele-utilizate-de-institutiile-financiare-raportoare-f3000-dac2crs-si-f3000-fatca-au-fost-modificate-noi-instructiuni-de-completare-22150/); [BZI](https://www.bzi.ro/anaf-reaminteste-termenul-de-depunere-a-formularului-f3000-15-mai-2025-5198585).
- Inference: checking self-certifications against KYC data (address, phone, place of birth and other indicia) is a natural rules job.

### 5.3 Corporate KYC: ONRC and beneficial-owner discrepancies (Law 129/2019)
- Reporting entities, including banks, must notify ONPCSB and ONRC of **any discrepancy between the ONRC beneficial-owner register and the beneficial-owner data they hold.** The notice goes out with a qualified e-signature to a dedicated ONRC address, and ONRC then records a provisional annotation.
- Sources: [Fiscalitatea, draft procedure](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/registrul-beneficiarilor-reali-si-inscrierearadierea-mentiunilor-provizorii-procedura-a-fost-pusa-in-dezbatere-publica-20696/); [Ordin MJ 7324/2020 on ONPCSB](https://www.onpcsb.ro/pdf/Ordinul%20MJ%207324%20din%202020.pdf). [1–2 src, secondary summaries; check the current Law 129/2019 text.]
- **This is literally "reconcile records and identify discrepancies".**
- Typical corporate documents on refresh: recent ONRC certificate, shareholder structure, administrators ([Capital.ro, May 2026](https://www.capital.ro/multi-romani-risca-sa-ramana-fara-acces-la-banii-din-cont-banca-trimite-notificari-verificarile-pe-care-clientii-trebuie-sa-le-faca.html)).

### 5.4 New law from 2026: companies must hold a bank account (Law 239/2025)
- Published in Monitorul Oficial no. 1160 on 15 Dec 2025. From **1 Jan 2026** companies must hold a payment account in Romania or at the Treasury, with fines of up to 10,000 lei.
- Sources disagree on whether it applies to all companies or only newly incorporated ones. PFAs and IIs are not obliged to hold an account but must accept modern payment methods.
- Sources: [Fiscalitatea](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/legea-2392025-firmele-amendate-cu-pana-la-10000-lei-in-2026-daca-nu-au-cel-putin-un-cont-bancar-24589/); [Contabilul](https://contabilul.manager.ro/a/29736/legea-2392025-lista-principalelor-masuri-fiscale-aplicabile-din-2026.html) [2 src].
- Context: in 2022 ANAF said about **300,000 of 1.6M firms** had no bank account ([Economedia, Sep 2022](https://economedia.ro/heius-anaf-una-din-patru-firme-din-romania-nu-are-cont-bancar-sunt-aproximativ-300-000-de-firme-care-nu-au-cont-bancar-dintr-un-total-de-16-milioane-de-firme.html)).
- Inference: this means more SME onboarding files, and fits the bank's reported **+70% new SME clients in H1 2026**.

### 5.5 Consumer credit rules
- **Law 243/2024** has capped interest rates since 11 Nov 2024 ([Puterea.ro, 1 Feb 2026](https://www.puterea.ro/?p=550200)) [1 src].
- **CCD2 (Directive (EU) 2023/2225)** had to be transposed by 20 Nov 2025 and applies from **20 Nov 2026**. Romania has a Ministry of Economy **draft** (Feb 2026), and the EU opened an infringement procedure. **Status as of today is unconfirmed.** The draft would require:
  - creditworthiness assessed on information that is "necesare, suficiente și proporționale"
  - a restructuring offer before any forced enforcement
  - fees fixed in lei and capped (for example, 150 lei for consumer-requested services)
  - a ban on unsolicited credit sales
- Sources: [Puterea.ro](https://www.puterea.ro/?p=550200); [SpotMedia, infringement](https://spotmedia.ro/stiri/economie/infringement-ue-pentru-romania-din-cauza-intarzierilor-in-aplicarea-noilor-reguli-pentru-credite-si-servicii-financiare).

### 5.6 State-backed lending programmes
- **Noua Casă** continues in 2026 with an FNGCIMM guarantee of 50–60%:
  - loans up to €70k need a 5% down payment
  - loans up to €140k need a 15% down payment
  - the buyer must own no other home
  - the property cannot be sold for 5 years
  - unfinished buildings and properties without complete legal documentation are not eligible
  - 13 banks took part in the 2025 programme
- Sources: [Capital.ro](https://www.capital.ro/programul-noua-casa-2026-romanii-care-pot-beneficia-de-ajutor-de-la-stat-conditiile-pentru-acordarea-banilor.html); [Wall-Street.ro](https://www.wall-street.ro/articol/Finante-Banci/258342/credit-prima-casa-noua-casa-conditii.html); [Economedia](https://economedia.ro/?p=254684). **I did not confirm whether Raiffeisen participates in 2026.**
- **IMM Invest:** Raiffeisen granted **2,333 loans worth €592M in 2022** ([Economica.net](https://www.economica.net/raiffeisen-bank-a-acordat-anul-trecut-2-333-de-credite-in-valoare-de-592-de-milioane-de-euro-in-cadrul-programului-garantat-de-stat-imm-invest_646532.html)) [1 src]. IMM Plus succeeded it in 2024 ([Bursa](https://www.bursa.ro/fngcimm-implements-four-of-the-six-sub-programmes-of-imm-plus-88243254)). Participation in 2025–26 is not confirmed.
- Inference: these programmes are eligibility checklists, a classic "validate an application against requirements" task.

### 5.7 Credit bureau and credit register
- Centrala Riscului de Credit (CRC): monthly reporting of exposures above **20,000 lei** ([BT FAQ](https://intreb.bancatransilvania.ro/restanta/)). A new BNR regulation reportedly restructures the CRC into six files; I could not confirm its date ([Puterea.ro](https://www.puterea.ro/?p=527522)).
- Biroul de Credit queries require signed consent. Raiffeisen was fined in 2022 partly for queries made without it (§1).

### 5.8 Garanti BBVA integration (inference)
Absorbing a bank with its own client, KYC and loan files (deal facts in §3.1) normally means large data-migration and reconciliation work, such as mapping products, deduplicating clients who bank at both, and refreshing KYC. **This is my inference; no source describes the integration plan.**

**Not researched:** e-Factura, dare în plată volumes, Biroul de Credit procedure changes in 2025–26.

## 6. Top 8 operational pain points, ranked by evidence strength × fit for a document-and-rules AI agent

**Scoring**
- **Evidence (Ev):** H = regulator or primary source specific to the bank. M = a mix of bank-specific and generic or anecdotal. L = generic only.
- **Fit:** how well a demo of "input → apply rules → outcome or exception" works on synthetic data.
- **Overlap:** risk of duplicating existing Raiffeisen AI (§3.2).

| # | Pain point | Ev | Fit | Recency | Overlap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Controlling staff-made changes to client data and products | H | H | Nov 2024 | Low |
| 2 | Checking credit files from DSA agents and brokers for completeness and consent | H | H | 2022 fine + Jun 2026 job | Low–Med |
| 3 | Reconciling access rights against regulatory registers | H (primary) | H | Nov 2024 | Low |
| 4 | Processing garnishments and computing payable amounts | H for volume, L for Raiffeisen-specific | H | 2023–2025 | Low |
| 5 | KYC refresh and account-restriction triage, including corporate KYC and UBO discrepancies | M | H | 2025–2026 | Med (SME doc reading) |
| 6 | Mandated remediation: recalculation and refund eligibility | H (old) | M–H | 2021–2024, plus 2026 ROBOR risk | Low |
| 7 | Complaint and request triage with data-leak guardrails | M | H | 2022 + 2026 | Med (ARI is internal only) |
| 8 | Triage of AML transaction-monitoring alerts | M | M | 2023/24 + 2026 jobs | Med–High (existing analytics) |

1. **Controlling staff-made changes to client data and products.** In Nov 2024 ANSPDCP *ordered* Raiffeisen to run documented, periodically tested controls on every add or update of client personal data, with the client notified and agreeing. Cases behind the order: an employee changed contact details, opened accounts and products, and used cards without consent from 2015 to 2023; in a 2022 case a client's phone number was replaced with an employee's ([e-juridic](https://e-juridic.manager.ro/articole/clienti-cu-bani-retrasi-din-conturi-si-credite-nesolicitate-banca-din-romania-buna-de-plata-dupa-multiple-incalcari-gdpr-29973.html); [Wall-Street.ro](https://www.wall-street.ro/articol/Finante-Banci/312096/raiffeisen-bank-este-amendata-cu-20-000-de-euro-pentru-ca-niste-angajati-au-folosit-datele-clientilor.html); [Profit.ro](https://profit.ro/stiri/raiffeisen-bank-romania-a-fost-amendata-cu-28-000-euro-pentru-mai-multe-incidente-privind-securitatea-datelor-clientilor-20923023)). The regulator has effectively written the rulebook. Demo: an agent reads a change log plus the supporting request documents, then flags changes with no client-initiated request, a new phone or email that matches an employee or another client, a product opened without a signed form, or no notification sent.

2. **Checking credit files from DSA agents and brokers for completeness and consent.** A 2026 job posting describes processing broker and DSA credit files, chasing missing documents and drafting OPC, derogation and memo approval documents ([Hipo](https://www.hipo.ro/locuri-de-munca/locuri_de_munca/268954/Raiffeisen-Bank/Ofiter-Procesare-Vanzari-Directe-DSA-Sud-Est)). The 2022 GDPR fine covered Biroul de Credit queries without signed consent, prescoring for an external broker, and loans nobody requested via a processor ([Profit.ro](https://profit.ro/stiri/raiffeisen-bank-romania-a-fost-amendata-cu-28-000-euro-pentru-mai-multe-incidente-privind-securitatea-datelor-clientilor-20923023)). Volumes are rising: unsecured loans +19% and new secured lending +57% in H1 2026 ([BVB](https://bvb.ro/infocont/infocont26/RBRO27A_20260730173230_RBRO-Rezultate-Financiare-2026-Q2-RO.pdf)). Demo: check a file against a checklist covering ID validity, income proof, signed bureau consent dated before the query, and Noua Casă or CCD2 criteria, then output "ready" or a list of missing items and exceptions.

3. **Reconciling access rights against regulatory registers.** ASF sanctioned the bank because people with rights in the fund-distribution app had not been notified to ASF. The bank handles 1,025, 725 and 330 notifications a year (2022, 2023, H1 2024) ([ASF Decision 1168/2024](https://asfromania.ro/uploads/articole/attachments/67482756896ef352823949.pdf)). This is the brief's "reconcile records and identify discrepancies" almost word for word. Demo: join an app-user export, the HR roster and the ASF-notified list, then flag active users who were never notified, notified leavers, and dormant accounts. A primary source, but the scale is smaller.

4. **Processing garnishments and computing payable amounts.** In H1 2025 alone, ANAF sent banks 1.08M garnishment orders and 1.13M lifting decisions via e-Popriri, system-wide. Since Oct 2023 banks must also compute and report the amounts actually payable across accounts ([ANAF H1 2025](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/Informatii_R/18.08.2025%20-%20Raport%20de%20activitateANAF_2025S1_V7.pdf); [OPANAF 626/2023](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/OPANAF_626_2023.pdf)). Raiffeisen runs popriri from its Brașov operations centre ([Bursa](https://www.bursa.ro/raiffeisen-bank-a-inaugurat-centrul-operational-brasov-07758120)). The strongest volume evidence, but **no Raiffeisen-specific error was found**, so pitch it as a volume and efficiency case, not a "you make mistakes" case. Demo: parse a garnishment order (ANAF or bailiff), match the debtor to accounts, apply the order of priority and protected amounts *as configurable rules*, and flag exceptions such as a name or CNP mismatch, multiple creditors, a suspension, or a lifting order arriving.

5. **KYC refresh and account-restriction triage, including corporate KYC and UBO discrepancies.** Evidence:
   - Raiffeisen clients report locks tied to data updates (Trustpilot 2025–26, anecdotal) ([Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/raiffeisen.ro)).
   - The bank was hiring KYC approval and monitoring specialists in 2026 (§4).
   - Banks restrict accounts automatically after the deadline (generic: [Capital.ro, May 2026](https://www.capital.ro/multi-romani-risca-sa-ramana-fara-acces-la-banii-din-cont-banca-trimite-notificari-verificarile-pe-care-clientii-trebuie-sa-le-faca.html)).
   - Law 129/2019 requires reporting discrepancies against the ONRC beneficial-owner register ([Fiscalitatea](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/registrul-beneficiarilor-reali-si-inscrierearadierea-mentiunilor-provizorii-procedura-a-fost-pusa-in-dezbatere-publica-20696/)).
   - Law 239/2025 adds SME onboarding volume ([Fiscalitatea](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/legea-2392025-firmele-amendate-cu-pana-la-10000-lei-in-2026-daca-nu-au-cel-putin-un-cont-bancar-24589/)).

   Demo: compare a refreshed KYC pack (ID, ONRC certificate, UBO declaration) against core-system data and the register, then output "auto-clear", "request a specific document" or "escalate". Overlap risk: the bank already reads SME documents, so the value has to come from cross-checking.

6. **Mandated remediation: recalculation and refund eligibility.** The bank itself said the ANPC-mandated recalculation for about 14,000 restructured loans was "relativ complex" and done "manual" ([Stiripesurse, 2021](https://www.stiripesurse.ro/raiffeisen-bank-ordinul-anpc-a-fost-implementat-inca-din-2014-calculam-si-restituim-banii-clientilor_1976579.html)). Refunds on a separate case still had €1.52M unpaid in 2024 ([Economica.net](https://www.economica.net/?p=731029)). The 2026 ROBOR decision may trigger civil claims ([Economedia](https://economedia.ro/seful-consiliului-concurentei-despre-investigatia-pe-robor-dupa-ce-publicam-decizia-in-baza-ei-se-pot-face-cereri-in-procese-civile-cum-ar-putea-oamenii-sa-ceara-despagubiri-care-este-practica-e.html)). Demo: take the contract terms and repayment history, apply the court or ANPC rule, compute the over-collection, and flag cases needing a human, such as closed accounts or heirs. Strong but older evidence, and heavier on calculation.

7. **Complaint and request triage with data-leak guardrails.** In 2022 the bank was fined partly because a **complaint reply carried another client's documents** and emails went to the wrong recipients ([Profit.ro](https://profit.ro/stiri/raiffeisen-bank-romania-a-fost-amendata-cu-28-000-euro-pentru-mai-multe-incidente-privind-securitatea-datelor-clientilor-20923023)). Reviews complain of slow support (Trustpilot 2026). CSALB requests rose 40% system-wide in H1 2026 ([ZF](https://www.zf.ro/banci-si-asigurari/romanii-au-transmis-la-csalb-in-s1-2026-peste-2-000-de-cereri-de-23195619)). Demo: classify the request, extract the identifiers, route it, draft a reply, and run a guardrail that checks every attachment and identifier belongs to the same client before sending.

8. **Triage of AML transaction-monitoring alerts.** 2026 postings describe analysts who "Analizeaza alertele…, Documenteaza rezultatele…, Escaladeaza… MLRO", using Excel ([Hipo](https://www.hipo.ro/locuri-de-munca/locuri_de_munca/271835/)). BNR reportedly fined the bank over badly parameterised monitoring applications ([Puterea.ro](https://www.puterea.ro/?p=342703), [1 src]). Demo: enrich an alert, apply typology rules, and produce a draft case narrative with a disposition (close or escalate). Ranked last because the bank already runs "unusual transaction" analytics, and this AML evidence is the weakest of the eight.

**Cross-cutting pitch line, built from the bank's own words:** AI "poate pregăti o decizie… dar nu ar trebui să aibă ultimul cuvânt" ([SpotMedia](https://spotmedia.ro/stiri/economie/zdenek-romanek-ceo-raiffeisen-bank-ai-poate-pregati-o-decizie-bancara-dar-nu-ar-trebui-sa-aiba-ultimul-cuvant)). Design every demo so the agent prepares the case and flags exceptions, and a human makes the decision.
